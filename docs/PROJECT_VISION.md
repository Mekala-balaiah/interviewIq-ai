# Project Vision — InterviewIQ AI

## Executive Summary

InterviewIQ AI is an **enterprise-grade AI-powered recruitment and interview intelligence platform** that transforms how organizations discover, evaluate, and hire top talent.

The platform addresses a critical gap in the HR technology market: the fragmentation between ATS systems, interview platforms, coding assessments, and candidate analytics. InterviewIQ AI unifies all these touchpoints under a single intelligent platform powered by large language models, real-time analytics, and automation.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern recruitment is broken:

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Manual resume screening is slow | Average time-to-hire is 36–42 days |
| Inconsistent interview questions | Hiring bias and poor candidate quality |
| No unified candidate analytics | Recruiters lack data-driven insights |
| Technical assessments are disconnected | Candidates struggle with fragmented experiences |
| Poor candidate communication | 65% of candidates get no feedback |
| No AI assistance for recruiters | Recruiter productivity is low |

---

## Vision Statement

> **"To become the world's most intelligent recruitment platform — where every hiring decision is backed by AI-driven insights, every candidate gets a fair evaluation, and every recruiter becomes 10x more effective."**

---

## Target Users

### Primary Users
1. **Candidates** — Job seekers uploading resumes, taking AI interviews, completing coding assessments
2. **Recruiters** — HR professionals managing pipelines, screening candidates, generating interview questions
3. **HR Managers** — Team leads overseeing hiring metrics, approvals, compliance

### Secondary Users
4. **Company Admins** — Platform administrators managing roles, settings, integrations
5. **System Admins** — Super admins managing multi-tenant configuration
